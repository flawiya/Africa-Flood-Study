2013 Long Rains - Western Kenya & Rift Valley floods

SWI = 56.68

Impact: 96 deaths, 140,000+ displaced (28,735 households); 13,895 acres farmland destroyed; Rift Valley lakes began decade-long expansion

2018 Long Rains - Nationwide flooding (40 of 47 counties)

SWI = 54.53

Impact: 150-186 deaths, 310,000+ displaced; Solai Dam collapse killed 47; 280+ schools damaged; ~21,000 ha maize destroyed

2020 Long Rains - Lake Victoria 63-year record high; basin-wide flooding

SWI = 72.71

Impact: 200,000+ displaced across East Africa; thousands of hectares cropland submerged in Kisumu County; lake rose 1.2m in under a year
