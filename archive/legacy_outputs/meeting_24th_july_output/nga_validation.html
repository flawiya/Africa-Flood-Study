Documented Flood Events & Index Values

2010 Northern Nigeria floods - Dam releases & Jigawa state flooding

SWI = 43.91

Impact: Millions affected; forced dam gate openings displaced large populations in Jigawa and other northern states; Lagos flash floods

References:
[1] ReliefWeb - Nigeria: Floods - Sep 2010
[2] Wikipedia - 2010 West Africa floods

2012 Nigeria Mega-Flood - Worst flooding in 40-50 years

SWI = 45.17

Impact: 363 deaths, 7.7M affected, 2.1-2.3M displaced; 32 of 36 states affected; 600,000 homes damaged; N2.6 trillion losses

References:
[1] ReliefWeb - Nigeria: Floods - Jul 2012 (FL-2012-000138-NGA)
[2] Wikipedia - 2012 Nigeria floods
[3] GFDRR - Nigeria Post-Disaster Needs Assessment 2012

2015 Nigeria floods - 11 states affected

SWI = 44.11

Impact: ~53 deaths, ~100,000 displaced; compounded by broken dams

References:
[1] ReliefWeb - Nigeria: Floods - 2015

2022 Nigeria floods - Most devastating in a decade

SWI = 46.38

Impact: 600-665 deaths, 4.4M affected, 1.4-2.4M displaced; 33 of 36 states; 200,000+ homes damaged; 560,000+ ha farmland destroyed

References:
[1] ReliefWeb - Nigeria: Floods - Aug 2022 (FL-2022-000271-NGA)
[2] Wikipedia - 2022 Nigeria floods

2024 Nigeria floods - Alau Dam collapse & nationwide flooding

SWI = 48.44

Impact: ~1,231 deaths, ~3M affected, ~890,000 displaced; Alau Dam collapse caused mass displacement in Maiduguri, Borno State

References:
[1] ReliefWeb - Nigeria: Floods - 2024
[2] Wikipedia - 2024 Nigeria floods
