# Pan-African Option 2 (5-Day Continuous Basis) Historical Flood Validation Registry (2008–2024)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Executive Summary & Parametric Verification**  
> This registry documents and validates all historical flood events triggered under **Option 2** (**95th Percentile + 85% Absolute SWI Floor** on a **5-Day Continuous Streak Basis**) across benchmark African agricultural regions (2008–2024).  
> All triggered district groups in a single crop year are cross-verified against official ground-truth disaster reports from the **United Nations Office for the Coordination of Humanitarian Affairs (UN OCHA / ReliefWeb)**, **World Bank / GFDRR Post-Disaster Needs Assessments (PDNA)**, **Copernicus Climate Change Service**, **World Health Organization (WHO)**, **FEWS NET**, and verified disaster archives.
